FAQ

Good questions.

How can I attend?

Register to attend in person for $50 on Zeffy, or watch the speaker tracks free on the live stream. Subscribe to the Simply Cyber Con and Simply Cyber Cafe YouTube channels to get notified when we go live. Recordings stay available on YouTube.

What's included?

In person: the full program with access to all main activities, plus breakfast and lunch on Day 1 and Day 2. Virtual: live stream access to the session talks and connection with the community in the SC Con Discord.

Where should I stay?

The conference hotel is Tides Folly Beach. See Stay on location for the group rate link and other nearby options.

What are the transportation options?

Rideshare may not be frequent on Folly Beach, so reserve in advance. Tides Folly Beach suggests Charlestowne Express at (843) 865-6686. All conference events are at the hotel or within walking distance.

What time is check-in?

Check-in starts at 8:00 AM during breakfast on Sunday, November 8. If you arrive after breakfast, find a conference representative and we'll get you sorted.

What time does the conference end?

The conference officially ends at 5:00 PM on Monday, November 9. There's an unofficial happy hour afterward at the con hotel or a local spot within walking distance.

Is there a code of conduct?

Yes. Everyone at Simply Cyber Con, in person and online, agrees to our Code of Conduct. It also explains how to reach an organizer if something goes wrong.

Can I connect with other attendees before the con?

Yes. Join the official Simply Cyber Con Discord, open now through the event. Virtual attendees are encouraged to hang out there during the talks too.
